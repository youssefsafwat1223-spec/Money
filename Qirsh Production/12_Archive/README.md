## Why they were superseded rather than deleted

`QIRSH_RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md` was written during release *preparation*, when the
production Supabase project was assumed to be an existing one. It is factually
wrong on that central point now. Its migration, secrets and capability detail was
carried forward into the master runbook and the `02_Supabase/` and
`07_Cloud_Capabilities/` documents.

`OWNER_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` predates the audit closure and lists items since
completed. Keeping it visible would risk redoing finished work.

Both remain useful as a record of what was believed and when.
