one per locale/theme — five ad requests each, 20/20 geometry PASS.

## Verified in code, NOT on a device — deferred

**Entitlement and consent.** `test/features/ads/entitlement_consent_gate_test.dart`
drives the real `ReportEntitlementResolver` over a fake `ReferralService` and
the real eligibility chain, counting ad requests rather than watching widgets:

- eligible → 1 request; ad-free, lookup failure, null decision, signed-out → 0;
- nothing requested while either answer is still resolving;
- consent granted → 1, withheld → 0;
- earning ad-free or revoking consent mid-session DISPOSES an ad already held.

Mutation-checked: removing the entitlement check fails seven of twelve,
removing the consent check fails two.

**What that does not cover, and why it is deferred rather than missing:** the
real Supabase entitlement backend and the real UMP consent flow have never run
against these gates on a device. That needs a QA account and environment, which
is an owner action. Recorded as **deferred pre-rollout verification** — not a
blocker for development, and a blocker for rollout.
